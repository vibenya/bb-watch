# BB Quick Voice — current specification

The canonical contract is [BB Watch product 3.0](bb-watch-product-spec.md),
with [design](../design.md), [transitions/errors](bb-watch-transition-contract.md)
and the [current concept](../docs/design/voice-terminal-v8.png).

Two surfaces: recorder and Threads. Stop/save becomes red classic REC for append;
Enter sends. One lower thread/status component, no recording history or delivery
reader. Notification reply opens the same recorder, subject to platform proof.

Earlier [variants](archive/bb-watch-quick-voice-before-3.0.md) and Fable approvals
are historical. This is a behavioral specification, not implemented functionality.
